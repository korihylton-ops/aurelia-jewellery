Questions

Good to Know

How long does a bespoke commission take?

Typically 6-10 weeks from consult to collection, depending on complexity and stone sourcing.

Do you offer resizing and repairs?

Yes, in-house for any Aurelia piece, and most other jewellery too — bring it in for a free assessment.

Where do your stones come from?

All diamonds are conflict-free and traceable; coloured stones are sourced from small, vetted suppliers.

Can you remake an inherited piece?

Yes — this is one of our most requested commissions. Bring the piece in and we'll talk through options.
